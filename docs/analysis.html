1. Design optimisation

Span, chord and battery are searched for maximum range at a required 25 m/s cruise. Constraints: thrust-to-weight ≥ 2, most-loaded rotor ≤ 75 % of maximum with any one rotor failed, stall ≤ 15 m/s, battery C-rate and pusher current. Lift-rotor power comes from the T-Motor bench table, pusher performance from APC's propeller data, structure masses from the CAD.
